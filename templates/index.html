          System Architecture

          How AgroScan AI Works

          1. Image Preprocessing

          224×224 RGB Normalization

          Uploaded leaf images are validated, converted to 3-channel RGB, bilinearly resized to 224×224 pixels, and normalized to floating-point tensors in [0.0, 1.0].

          2. Transfer Learning

          MobileNetV2 Feature Backbone

          Uses inverted residual blocks and linear bottlenecks pre-trained on ImageNet and fine-tuned with ImageDataGenerator augmentations on PlantVillage leaf classes.

          3. Softmax & Severity

          Multi-Class Pathology Scoring

          Outputs calibrated Top-3 softmax probabilities across Healthy, Early Blight, Late Blight, Bacterial Spot, and Powdery Mildew with canopy lesion area estimation.

          4. IPM Decision Support

          Organic & Chemical Protocols

          Maps each diagnosed pathogen to Integrated Pest Management (IPM) treatments, FRAC fungicide rotation guidelines, and mandatory expert validation disclaimers.
